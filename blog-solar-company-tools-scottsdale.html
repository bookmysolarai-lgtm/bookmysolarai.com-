Scottsdale is one of Arizona's most lucrative markets for residential solar — high homeownership rates, large single-family homes, high average electricity bills, and homeowners who are used to spending on premium home improvements. The opportunity is massive.

But capturing that opportunity requires more than just panels and installers. The Scottsdale solar companies winning right now have built lean, efficient tech stacks that handle lead response, appointment setting, project management, and customer communication automatically.

This guide covers the full solar tech stack recommended for Scottsdale-area installers in 2025.

The 5-Layer Solar Company Tech Stack

Layer 1: Lead Generation

Before you can convert leads, you need them. For Scottsdale solar companies, the highest-performing channels are:

  • Facebook & Instagram Ads — Targeting Scottsdale homeowners by zip code, homeownership status, and income. Standard for residential solar lead gen.
  • Google Local Service Ads — Appear at the very top of Google when Scottsdale homeowners search "solar company near me."
  • Google Business Profile — Free, essential for local SEO. If you haven't claimed and optimized your GBP listing for Scottsdale, do it today.
  • Referral programs — Scottsdale homeowners talk to their neighbors. A $500–$1,000 referral incentive program pays for itself quickly.

Layer 3: CRM & Pipeline Management

Once Stacy qualifies and books an appointment, your team needs a clean system to manage the pipeline from appointment to close to installation:

  • GoHighLevel — The most popular CRM among solar companies in Arizona. Handles pipeline, SMS, email follow-up sequences, and reputation management in one platform.
  • HubSpot — Better for companies with larger sales teams. More complex but more powerful at scale.
  • Salesforce Solar Cloud — Enterprise-grade. Best for companies doing 100+ installs per month.

Layer 4: Proposal & Design Tools

  • Aurora Solar — Industry standard for remote solar design and proposal generation. Creates professional, satellite-based proposals that close deals in-home.
  • Solargraf — Simpler alternative to Aurora with faster proposal generation.
  • Energy Toolbase — Best for battery storage + solar proposals, increasingly relevant in Scottsdale as APS rates push battery ROI.

Layer 5: Project Management & Post-Sale

  • JobNimbus — Purpose-built for solar roofing contractors. Handles permitting workflow, inspections, and milestone tracking.
  • Scoop Solar — Field operations management specifically for solar installers.
  • Sighten — Solar-specific project management with financing integration.
