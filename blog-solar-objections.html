The 5 Most Common Objections on a First Solar Call — And Exactly How to Handle Every One

March 2025 7 min read Solar Sales
📞

Whether it's your human caller or your AI agent making the first contact, every solar lead generates objections. The companies that train their systems to handle these objections confidently and consistently book dramatically more appointments than those who haven't.

Here are the 5 most common objections you'll hear on a first solar call — with exact scripts that work.

Objection #1

"I need to talk to my spouse / partner before booking."

This is the most common objection in residential solar. It's not a rejection — it's a stall. The goal is to invite both decision-makers to the appointment, not to overcome the need to consult a partner.

The Response

"That makes total sense — this is a household decision and it's great that you want your [spouse/partner] involved. Actually, these consultations go much better when both homeowners are there, because our advisor can answer both of your questions at once. What days work best for both of you? We can find a time that works for your whole household."

Objection #2

"I'm already getting quotes from other companies."

This is a great sign — it means the homeowner is a serious buyer. The job is to get your appointment on the calendar before or alongside theirs, not to argue why you're better.

The Response

"That's smart — you should absolutely compare options on a purchase this size. Most homeowners we work with get 2-3 quotes, and we're confident in what we bring to the table. All I'd ask is that you give us the same 45 minutes you're giving the others. When can we get on your calendar?"

Objection #3

"My roof is old / I need to replace it first."

This is a concern that can actually be addressed in-home. Many solar companies bundle roof replacement with solar installation — and financing makes it affordable.

The Response

"That comes up often and it's a great question to address. Our advisor can actually assess the roof during the same appointment — we work with homeowners in that situation regularly and there are usually some good options. It costs nothing to have the conversation. When works for you?"

Objection #4

"I'm not sure I can afford it / I don't have the money."

Most people don't realize solar is $0 down with monthly payments that are often less than their current electric bill. The job is to get to the numbers conversation in person.

The Response

"I completely understand the concern — that's actually the most common thing we hear before people learn how it works. The majority of our customers pay nothing upfront, and their monthly solar payment is less than what they currently pay the utility. Our advisor's whole job is to run the actual numbers for your home and show you whether it makes financial sense. Would you be open to seeing those numbers?"

Objection #5

"Now isn't a good time / I'm too busy."

This is a scheduling objection, not a "no." The solution is to make booking as frictionless as possible and offer around their schedule.

The Response

"Not a problem at all — the last thing I want to do is pressure you. Our consultations are about 45 minutes at a time that's completely convenient for you, including weekends and evenings. If I find something 2-3 weeks out, would that work better? What does your schedule generally look like?"
