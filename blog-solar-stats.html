12 Solar Lead Statistics Every Installer Needs to Know in 2025

March 2025 5 min read Industry Data
📊

Data doesn't lie. If you run a residential solar company and you're not obsessing over these numbers, you're leaving massive amounts of money on the table — every single day.

Here are 12 statistics that should fundamentally change how you think about lead response, solar sales, and your competitive position.

78%

Of solar deals go to the first company to respond

When a homeowner submits a solar inquiry, whoever reaches them first controls the conversation and the sale. Competition is a race — and most solar companies are losing it. — Velocify

47h

Average solar lead response time in the industry

The average solar company takes 47+ hours to contact a new lead. While a few companies have invested in fast response systems, the majority are still shockingly slow. — Solar Industry Benchmark Report

400%

Higher contact rate when calling within 60 seconds vs. 30 minutes

Not 10% higher. Not 50% higher. Four hundred percent higher. The 60-second window is the most valuable 60 seconds in solar sales. — Lead Response Management Study

21x

More likely to qualify a lead when reached within 5 minutes vs. 30 minutes

The difference between sub-5-minute contact and 30-minute contact isn't marginal — it's a 21x difference in outcome. Most solar companies operate closer to the 30-minute end. — Harvard Business Review

80%

Of lead conversion probability lost within the first 5 minutes

A lead's likelihood to convert drops 80% in just 5 minutes after they submit a form. By the time most solar companies call, the opportunity has already mostly evaporated. — HBR

$150

Average cost per inbound solar lead

Depending on your market and channel, solar leads typically cost $80–$300 each. Every lead that goes cold without contact is real money destroyed. At $150 CPL and 40% loss rate, 50 leads/month = $3,000/month wasted.

40%

Of inbound solar leads never receive a timely first contact

Four in ten leads that come in from ads or websites never get reached in time to convert. They go cold, move on, or sign with a faster competitor. — InsideSales.com

6x

More likely to have a meaningful conversation when contacted within 1 hour

Companies that follow up within 1 hour are 6x more likely to have a productive first conversation. Most solar companies aren't there yet. — InsideSales.com

35%

Of all solar inquiries come in on weekends

Saturday and Sunday generate roughly a third of all solar form submissions — when most sales teams are offline. Without automated weekend coverage, these leads are guaranteed to go cold by Monday.

$12K

Average residential solar deal value

At an average deal value of $10,000–$15,000, every lost lead isn't just a $150 cost. It's a $12,000 revenue miss — plus the referrals that closed customer would have sent.

3.2x

Higher close rate for leads contacted within 5 minutes vs. 60 minutes

Close rate — not just contact rate — is significantly higher for fast-response companies. Speed doesn't just get you in front of more leads, it correlates with higher overall conversion. — Velocify

Every single one of these stats points to the same conclusion: speed-to-lead is the single highest-leverage variable in your solar company's sales performance.
