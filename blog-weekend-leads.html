Picture this: it's Saturday afternoon, 2pm. A homeowner in Chandler, Arizona just got their APS bill — $387 for July electricity. They're at home, frustrated, and they pull up their phone and Google "solar companies near me." They fill out a quote request form on your website.

What happens next at most solar companies? Nothing. Until Monday morning at 9am when someone finally sees the lead in the CRM — 43 hours later.

That homeowner has already talked to two other companies who called on Saturday afternoon.

The Weekend Lead Problem by the Numbers

  • ⏰ Average Monday response time to a weekend lead: 4–12 hours after business opens

Average time before a weekend lead gets their first call from a solar company. By then, the lead has already moved on — to whoever did call on Saturday.

Why Weekend Leads Are Actually Your Highest-Intent Leads

Here's the cruel irony: weekend solar leads tend to be more motivated than weekday leads, not less. Here's why:

  • They just opened a big utility bill. Nothing drives solar intent like a $400 summer electric bill on a Saturday morning.
  • They have time to think about it. Weekday leads often submit forms during a lunch break or spare moment — they're busy. Weekend leads are home, thinking about their finances, with time to engage.
  • They're available for a conversation. When Stacy calls a weekend lead at 2:30pm Saturday, the homeowner is home and available. That same lead called Monday at 10am may be in a work meeting.
  • They're in a decision-making mindset. Weekends are when people make home improvement decisions. Solar is a home improvement purchase.
