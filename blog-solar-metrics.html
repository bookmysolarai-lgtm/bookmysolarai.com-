The Only 3 Metrics Your Solar Company Actually Needs to Track (And How to Improve Each One)

March 2025 6 min read ROI & Metrics
🎯

Most solar companies are tracking too many things and not paying close enough attention to the things that actually matter. Dashboards full of vanity metrics. Weekly reports nobody acts on.

Here's the truth: if you obsess over just three numbers — and work relentlessly to improve them — your solar business will outperform 90% of competitors in your market. These are the three metrics.

📊 Metric #1: Speed to Lead

Average time between lead form submission and first dial attempt

Industry Avg 47 hours Your Target Under 5 min

Why It Matters

Speed to lead is the single most impactful metric in your entire sales operation. Calling within 5 minutes makes you 21x more likely to qualify the lead. Calling within 60 seconds increases contact rates by 400%. Every improvement here creates compounding returns across every downstream metric.

How to Improve It

  • Short-term: Set up SMS/email alerts for new leads and require your team to call within 15 minutes during business hours.
  • Medium-term: Use a dialer that auto-dials new leads when they come in.
  • Long-term (best ROI): Deploy an AI agent (BookMySolarAI) that dials within 60 seconds automatically, 24/7, no human trigger required.

📞 Metric #2: Contact Rate

% of new leads that reach a meaningful conversation on any attempt

Industry Avg 18–25% Your Target 40%+

Why It Matters

Your contact rate tells you what percentage of your paid leads you're actually reaching. At an industry average of 18–25%, that means 75–82% of leads you paid for never have a real conversation with your company. Even getting to 40% contact rate with the same lead spend roughly doubles your pipeline.

How to Improve It

  • Call faster: Speed to lead is the #1 driver of contact rate. Improving Metric #1 automatically improves Metric #2.
  • Call more times: If you don't reach on the first attempt, call again 3 hours later, then next morning, then next day. Most companies stop after 1-2 attempts.
  • Call at the right times: 8–9am and 4–6pm local time have the highest answer rates. Weekends are underutilized.
  • Add SMS: A text message accompanying or following the call significantly increases your odds of making contact on that lead.

📅 Metric #3: Appointment Show Rate

% of booked appointments where the homeowner is actually present

Industry Avg 50–60% Your Target 75%+

Why It Matters

A no-show appointment isn't just a lost opportunity — it's wasted time for your closer, wasted fuel, and a direct cost to your business. Getting your show rate from 55% to 75% means your closers are seeing 36% more qualified homeowners with the same number of booked appointments.

How to Improve It

  • Pre-qualify harder: Appointments should only be booked with fully qualified homeowners. Unqualified bookings have higher no-show rates.
  • Confirm via SMS: Send an automated SMS confirmation immediately after booking, then 24 hours before, then 2 hours before the appointment.
  • Book sooner: Appointments booked 48–72 hours out show better than appointments booked 7+ days out.
  • Warm the relationship: A quick text or email from your closer before the appointment ("Looking forward to seeing you tomorrow, John!") builds accountability.
